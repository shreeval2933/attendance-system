Quick Setup & Testing Guide

1. Instant Local Testing (Zero Config): The system includes a built-in simulation engine with multi-tab real-time sync. You can open admin.html in one tab and student.html in another to submit attendance and watch it appear on the admin map immediately!

2. Connecting to Firebase: Open firebase-config.js and paste your Firebase project credentials into firebaseConfig. Firestore will instantly activate for real cloud sync.

3. Geolocation & Anti-Proxy: Both pages utilize the HTML5 Geolocation API. Make sure location permissions are allowed when prompted by your browser or smartphone.
